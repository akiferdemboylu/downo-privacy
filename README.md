# Privacy Policy for Downo

**Last updated:** October 2026

Downo ("we", "our", or "us") provides a simple, fast media downloader utility. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Downo does not collect, store, or sell any personal data (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts). 

### 2. Media Processing & Device Storage
Any media downloaded through Downo is stored locally on your device in your public Media/Downloads directory via the Android MediaStore API. Downo does not upload, copy, or retain your downloaded files on external servers.

### 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers, cookies, and IP addresses to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google's advertising policies.
For more information, please visit [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### 4. Children's Privacy
Downo does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
